### Added
- **Tencent HY-Motion 1.0 (open text-to-motion) + FlowHMR** (Animation & Mocap): open-weight text-to-3D human motion models (1.0B and 0.46B Lite, 30 December 2025, technical report arXiv 2512.23464) on a Diffusion Transformer with flow matching, trained on over 3,000 hours of motion, fine-tuned on 400 hours and refined with reinforcement learning; English prompts under 60 words return SMPL-H skeletal clips (22 joints, no hands) saved as FBX when the FBX SDK is present, through a batch CLI, a Gradio app or the Hugging Face Space. Licence is the Tencent HY-Motion 1.0 Community License: commercial use allowed, but not in the European Union, United Kingdom or South Korea, and products over 1 million monthly active users need a separate licence. README lists about 26 GB of VRAM (24 GB Lite) and the unsupported cases (hands, faces, non-humans, objects and scenes, multi-person, loops). FlowHMR (repository created 29 September 2026, Apache 2.0 code, about 680 stars; weights under a non-commercial research licence) is a video-to-motion model built on HY-Motion that the HY-Motion README added on 30 October 2026, with NPZ/FBX export and a 12 GB consumer-GPU claim; README edits on 13 November 2026 are what surfaced it. Tencent's SSAE figure (78.6% against 58.0% for MoMask) is written as a vendor benchmark; no efficiency figure set. The card had sat on the watch list as a pre-window candidate since 12 November. Sources: https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0 (README, News) ; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0/master/License.txt ; https://api.github.com/repos/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0 (created 29 Dec 2025, about 2,670 stars) ; https://api.github.com/repos/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0/commits (13 Nov README edits) ; https://huggingface.co/tencent/HY-Motion-1.0 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23464 (29 Dec 2025) ; https://hunyuan.tencent.com/motion ; https://github.com/flowhmr/flowhmr (README, licence section) ; https://api.github.com/repos/flowhmr/flowhmr (created 29 Sep 2026, Apache-2.0) ; https://huggingface.co/fafsaf1/flowhmr-0.46B (weights licence, 82.47% tracking success is FlowHMR's own figure) ; independent coverage: https://rits.shanghai.nyu.edu/ai/tencent-open-sources-hy-motion-1-0-a-billion-parameter-text-to-motion-ai-model/ and the MarkTechPost write-up (31 Dec 2025) relayed at https://www.pulse.bot/ai/news/tencent-released-tencent-hy-motion-10-a-billion-parameter-text-to-motion-model-built-on-the-diffusio-57ef69bc-0463-453b-9856-9270e4488b36/ ; third-party limitation summary https://magicshot.ai/?p=15212 ; community nodes https://github.com/jtydhr88/ComfyUI-HY-Motion1 (VRAM figures there are unverified and not used on the card).
