# Changelog

## 2026-11-02
Three cards added and one updated (92 cards). No new efficiency figures qualified; all four cards keep "No published figure yet".

- Added **Aura Autopilot** (Ramen) to QA & Playtesting. Open-source (Apache 2.0) playtesting agent announced 30 Oct 2026: an editor plugin for Unreal 5.4–5.8, Unity 6 and Godot 4.7 on Windows and macOS that launches Play-In-Editor, plays to a prompt (explore, test, verify, goal), keeps a per-project memory and writes a report; also usable from Claude Code or Codex through its MCP server. Ramen's whitepaper figures (1.7× faster than Claude Code, 7 of 7 tasks in one UE 5.8 game) are stated on the card as the vendor's own benchmark, not as an efficiency figure.
  - Primary: https://www.tryaura.dev/autopilot/ , https://github.com/ramenvr/autopilot , https://www.tryaura.dev/documentation/autopilot/
  - Independent: https://www.gaming.net/ramen-aura-autopilot-open-source-ai-game-testing/ , https://gamesbeat.com/?p=337836
- Added **SimplyStream Strata** (Wonder Interactive) to Engine Integration & Code. A build agent that compiles Unreal 5.8 (and, per the vendor, UE6-line) projects to WebAssembly and WebGPU, plays each build in Chrome on a cloud GPU in desktop and mobile render modes, and publishes a browser play link; driven from Claude Code, Codex or any command-running agent, a CLI or a Fab plugin, with early Unity support. Announced 22 Oct 2026. Pricing from the vendor's page: Free (2 runs a day), Indie $25/mo, Studio $250/mo, Enterprise custom. The 15-minute code-to-verified-build median is the vendor's own game and is labelled as a vendor figure. Borderline for scope (it is an agent-driven build and deploy pipeline rather than a content tool), so flagged for the owner's call.
  - Primary: https://simplystream.com , https://simplystream.com/pricing , https://simplystream.com/docs
  - Independent: https://gamesbeat.com/?p=337297 (22 Oct 2026), https://respawn.media/tech/hyod6-ii-uzhe-umeet-perenosit-igry-na-unreal-engine-5-v-brauzery (22 Oct 2026)
- Added **Fish Audio Drama 3** to Audio & Voice. Released 13 Oct 2026: a text-to-speech model for multi-character scenes with bracketed stage directions, paired per-word tags, up to 16 speaker tags per script, voice cloning and 83 languages. Open weights (35B mixture-of-experts, about 3B active) under the Fish Audio Community License v1.0: free for commercial use under US$500k annual revenue; above that, or to offer it as a hosted service, a licence from Fish Audio is required. The 93.2% Pronunciation Robustness score is Artificial Analysis' independent benchmark as reported in the launch post. API list price $50 per million characters after the launch discount ends 12 Nov (per the launch post and the independent review). "Takes vary between runs" is from a single review and is marked as such.
  - Primary: https://fish.audio/blog/fish-audio-drama-3/ , https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/drama-3 , https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/drama-3/raw/main/LICENSE
  - Independent: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/fish-audio-drama-3-review (14 Oct 2026), https://alphasignal.ai/news/fish-audio-s-drama-3-lets-developers-direct-ai-voice-like-a-human-actor
