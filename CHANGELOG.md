# Changelog

## 2026-10-29
One card added (90 cards), six cards updated. No efficiency figures changed; none of the new releases publishes a graded figure. All changes are on top of `main` (2026-10-13); the open branches update/2026-10-16 to update/2026-10-28 are not merged and are not duplicated here.

- Added **KeenFace** (KeenTools) to Rigging & Skinning. Launched 22 Oct 2026: a browser Studio and REST API that build a 3D head from a few photos or a multi-camera scan set in consistent MetaHuman topology, with 4K PBR maps and 51 ARKit blendshapes, de-haired to a bald base. Pay per head (150 credits, about $15; failed heads free; 450 starter credits). The topology-consistency claim is the vendor's; the limitations (no eyes/teeth in the MetaHuman export, demanding capture conditions) come from a documentation-based review that was not a hands-on test, and are marked "per one review". No efficiency figure is published.
  - Primary: https://keenface.ai · https://keenface.ai/pricing
  - Independent: https://www.awn.com/news/keentools-keenface-turns-photos-metahuman-ready-3d-heads (22 Oct 2026) · https://digitalproduction.com/2026/10/22/keenface-photos-3d-heads/ · https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/10/new-cg-software-you-may-have-missed-25-october-2026/
- Updated **Qwen Image / Qwen Image Edit 2509** → **Qwen-Image-2.1 / Qwen Image Edit 2509** (2D Art & Concept). Qwen-Image-2.1 (20 Sept 2026, 7B) generates and edits with up to 10 reference images, mask-guided local edits and RGBA layers; a Turbo 8-step checkpoint and Pro/Turbo APIs followed on 9 Oct 2026. Added the key licence change: 2.1 is under the Qwen Research License (non-commercial), unlike the Apache 2.0 Qwen-Image 1.0 and Edit 2509, so commercial use needs a separate licence from Alibaba. The ~16 GB VRAM figure is from reviewers' reports. Added two official links.
  - Primary: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-Image-2.1 · https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1
  - Independent (licence, release date, VRAM): https://www.eesel.ai/blog/qwen-image-2-1 · https://cellcog.ai/blog/qwen-image-2-1/
- Updated **FLUX.2 / FLUX.1 Kontext** → **FLUX 3 Image Klein / FLUX.2 / FLUX.1 Kontext** (2D Art & Concept). FLUX 3 Image Klein (15 Oct 2026) is a 5.6B open-weight model for text-to-image, editing and multi-reference generation (up to 10 references, bounding-box layout), in Base and distilled variants, with an INT8 ComfyUI build for 8 GB RTX cards. Licence: FLUX Kommunity License, free for commercial use under $5M annual revenue; larger studios need a paid self-hosted licence or the API. BFL's benchmark lead is marked as an internal evaluation. Added the "Open" tag and two official links.
  - Primary: https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein · https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux-3-image-klein
