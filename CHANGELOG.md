# Changelog

## 2026-10-28
Six listed tools updated to their current versions; no cards added or removed (still 89). No new efficiency figures qualified.

- **NVIDIA DLSS 4.5 → DLSS 5 / 4.5** (Rendering). DLSS 5 3D-Guided Neural Rendering shipped 3 Sept 2026 in NBA 2K27 on RTX 50 Series; NVIDIA's 13 Oct update says the new model halves GPU overhead (vendor claim). Added the 50–60% frame-rate cost NVIDIA quoted to press (independent report). The existing +128% FPS bench figure is relabelled as DLSS 4.5.
  Sources: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/nba-2k27-dlss-5-3d-guided-neural-rendering-geforce-game-ready-driver/ · https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/dlss-5-faster-ai-model-hogwarts-legacy-rtx-spark-laptops/ · https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/technologies/dlss/ · https://www.notebookcheck.net/DLSS-5-release-date-confirmed-but-be-ready-for-a-massive-performance-loss.1385149.0.html
- **Kling 3.0 → Kling 4.0** (Trailers & UA). Launched 15 Oct 2026: 30 s single-pass clips, up to 10 keyframes and 15 reference assets. API, 4K and 10-bit HDR output marked "coming soon (reported)"; the $0.10/s price is kept but attributed to the 3.0 API.
  Sources: https://bernama.com/en/press/news.php?id=2622471 (Kuaishou release) · https://kling.ai · https://fal.ai/learn/tools/what-is-kling-4-0 · https://letsdatascience.com/news/kling-ai-opens-kling-40-to-all-users-d9dc6422
- **Meshy 6 → Meshy 7.1** (3D Modeling). Meshy 7 GA 10 Aug 2026; 7.1 (10 Sept 2026) adds Ultra 4K geometry at 4096³. Noted that Ultra 2K/4K and downloads need a paid plan and Ultra 4K is single-view only. Meshy's Detail Richness ranking is its own benchmark and was not used as a figure.
  Sources: https://www.meshy.ai/blog/meshy-7-1-launch · https://www.meshy.ai/blog/meshy-7-image-to-3d-geometry-alignment · https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/text-to-3d
- **Tripo 3D** (3D Modeling). Added P2.0 (21 Sept 2026): native quad meshes of 500–25k faces, up to four face-count variants per prompt, Mesh Edit. Added a con from an independent review (method undisclosed; GLB export may triangulate).
  Sources: https://www.webull.com/news/15619871280677888 (Tripo release via PR Newswire) · https://www.tripo3d.ai · https://digitalproduction.com/2026/09/29/tripo-p2-0-put-quad-mesh-generation-to-the-test/
- **Inworld TTS-1.5 → Inworld Realtime TTS-2** (Audio & Voice). TTS-2 GA 31 Aug 2026 with a Flash variant. Price changed from the old $5–10 to $25 per million characters on demand ($15 Flash); latency figures replaced with Inworld's stated 90 ms / 50 ms P90 (vendor).
  Sources: https://inworld.ai/blog/realtime-tts-2 · https://inworld.ai/tts
- **ElevenLabs** (Audio & Voice). Noted Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo (28 Sept 2026, 90+ languages). No figure added; the 150 ms latency claim is ElevenLabs' own test.
  Sources: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v4 · https://datanorth.ai/news/elevenlabs-releases-eleven-v4-and-eleven-v4-turbo
