How to read the efficiency figures

Every number applies to one step of the work, not a whole project. The best studio-reported data (2025–26 case studies) shows step-level gains of 40–80% shrinking to 10–20% across a full project once review, rework and the steps without AI are counted.

No published figure

Every number on this page comes from a studio report, an independent test or the vendor, and the card says which. Where nobody has published a figure, the card says so. Pilot the tool and log your own before-and-after time.

Before you adopt a tool

  • Check the commercial licence and plan tier.
  • Confirm the client contract allows AI at that stage.
  • Keep client IP off cloud tools you haven't approved.
  • Log the provenance of anything players will see (Steam, China and EU disclosure rules).
